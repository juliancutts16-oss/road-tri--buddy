  • AWTY is a road-trip app for children, set up and looked after by a parent or carer.

  • The free app costs nothing. The paid versions are monthly subscriptions through Google Play, and you can cancel them there at any time.
  • We check everything AI writes for AWTY before a child hears it, but no check is perfect. If anything doesn't feel right, please tell us.

Agreeing to these terms

By downloading or using AWTY, you agree to these terms. AWTY is meant to be set up and used by an adult (a parent or carer, aged 18 or over) for the children travelling with them. If you let a child use AWTY, you're responsible for their use of it. How we handle information is explained in our privacy policy.

Using AWTY safely

  • AWTY is for passengers. The driver must never use it, or be distracted by it, while driving.
  • Keep children safely belted in. Nothing in AWTY should ever be a reason to undo a seatbelt or move about in the car.
  • Journey times, distances and maps are estimates to keep children company on the way. Don't rely on AWTY to navigate: use a proper navigation app or road signs.
  • Keep your parent PIN to yourself, so the grown-up settings stay behind it.

The free app and the paid versions

  • £2.99 a month and, later, £5.99 a month are subscriptions bought through Google Play. What each one includes is shown on our home page and in the app before you buy.
  • Subscriptions renew each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in Google Play; the paid features then stay until the end of the month you've paid for, and the free app keeps working.

Fun facts and stories written by AI

In the paid versions, some fun facts (and, in the £5.99 version, stories and answers) are written by AI. AI can still get a fact wrong, so please treat facts as fun rather than as a reference. If anything isn't right, use Settings → Report a problem and we'll look at it.
