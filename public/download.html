Before your first trip

Three things to know

1

It's for Android

AWTY comes to Google Play first.

2

Open it once on Wi-Fi

The first time the phone is on Wi-Fi, Emma, the storyteller voice, downloads by herself (about 126 MB). After that she works anywhere, with no signal. If you'd rather use mobile data, tap "Download Emma now" in Settings → Sound.

3

Set it up, then hand it back

The first time AWTY opens, it says hello, then a short "Safety & privacy" card lets you choose what AWTY may do. You also pick a parent PIN. Then type where you're going and tap "Start Journey & Lock Screen".

The free app needs no sign-up. The £2.99 version will be a monthly subscription through Google Play, which you can cancel there at any time. See what's in each version.
