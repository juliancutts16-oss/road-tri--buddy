For children

Here is what AWTY does with information, in a few words:

  • Your name stays on this phone. We never send it anywhere.
  • A grown-up chooses what AWTY is allowed to do. You can ask them about it any time.
  • When you see the green Location on sign, AWTY is using the phone to know where the car is, so your car on the screen can move with the real one.
  • AWTY only listens if a grown-up has switched it on, and it never keeps a recording of your voice.
  • There are no adverts, and we never sell anything about you.

The short version

  • AWTY is a road-trip companion for children, set up and controlled by a parent or carer.
  • We don't show adverts, we don't track children, and we never sell data.
  • Children's names, settings, stats and saved trips are kept on your phone, not on our servers.
  • Some features need to send limited information online. A parent chooses which ones when first opening the app, and can change this at any time in Settings → Data & services.
  • The microphone is off unless a parent switches on "Listen for answers". We never record or store voice audio.

What stays on your phone

Children's names, age group, interests, the travel buddy's name, car and house designs, settings, trip stats, saved routes and backups are stored in the app on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Parent controls → Clear everything on this phone, removes them.

If a parent turns these off, AWTY still works using built-in stories, demo or timed routes, and on-phone answer checking.

Speech recognition and location on the phone

When "Listen for answers" is on, speech is turned into words by your phone's own speech service (on Android this is provided by Google; on iPhone by Apple), under their privacy terms. AWTY does not receive or keep any audio.

Location is a separate choice that starts switched off. If a parent switches it on, the phone's GPS position is used on the device to move the car along the route, and a green Location on sign shows on the children's screen whenever it is being used. GPS stops when the trip is paused or finished. We don't store location history. With location off, the car moves by the journey time instead, and you type the starting point.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, no advertising ID or device identifiers, no profiling.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information for marketing.
  • No voice recordings, and no children's names or location history on our servers.
  • No accounts for children.

Usage statistics: seeing and deleting them

Usage stats are only collected if a parent switches on "Help improve AWTY". They are sent to our own server, not to any analytics company. In Settings → Data & services → What we collect you can see, in plain English, exactly what has been recorded recently, and tap Delete my usage data to remove everything stored for your installation from our server; the app then uses a new random ID. Switching the choice off also deletes it. Only the AWTY team can see the combined totals, on a private page protected by a secret key.

Our service providers

  • Netlify: hosts the app and our server functions, and stores usage stats, issue reports and story reports (Netlify Blobs). Like most hosts, it keeps short-term technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security. We don't store IP addresses with usage stats.

Your rights

You can change the app's privacy choices at any time, delete everything stored on the phone from inside the app, and contact us at [privacy email address] to ask about, correct or delete any information we hold. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Safety

AWTY is for passengers. The driver should never use the app while driving.

Every AI-written story part is checked before it is read aloud: it is written to rules for the child's age group, then checked for unsuitable words, for reading level, and by a separate AI review. Anything that doesn't pass is rewritten once or replaced by the app's built-in story. Parents can read the whole story in Settings → Read the story and report any part, which hides it straight away. Interests typed in that aren't suitable are left out, and landmarks such as pubs, cemeteries, prisons and hospitals are never used as story stops.
