Set the destination, lock the screen and hand it back. The free app has all of this, with no sign-up.

A moving map shows how far there is to go, so "are we there yet?" answers itself.

Emma, a friendly British voice, reads out 200 built-in fun facts at the stars on the map. They live on the phone, so they work with no signal.

Our promise to parents

Built around the grown-up in the front seat

These rules come first in every change we make to AWTY. If a new idea would break one, we don't build it.

Names stay on the phone

Your children's names are never sent to our server or to any AI. The phone fills them in by itself.

The microphone stays off

It only switches on if a parent turns it on in Settings.

Grown-up bits need your PIN

Unlocking the trip screen, links that leave the app, sharing and privacy choices all ask for your PIN first.

Every story is checked

Stories and facts written by AI go through our safety check before your child hears a word.

Names stay private out loud too

In Free and £2.99, Emma's voice is made on the phone itself. No voice we use will ever be sent your children's names.

You choose what's shared

A clear privacy screen asks you first, and asks again whenever anything changes.

Help

Quick answers

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Choose a no-signal trip, set how long the drive is, and the car moves by the clock. Emma's fun facts are stored on the phone, so she keeps reading them.

Why does Emma need Wi-Fi?

Emma's voice downloads once, by herself, the first time the phone is on Wi-Fi. After that she works anywhere.

I've forgotten my PIN

Press and hold "Forgot PIN?" for 10 seconds, then choose a new one. Holding it that long keeps little fingers out.
