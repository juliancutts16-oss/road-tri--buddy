The road ahead

What we're working on next. We'll add each one when it's ready and safe, not before. Every new idea has to keep our promise to parents, or we don't build it.

1

Launch on Google Play

Next stop

The free app and the £2.99 version, with games, your own car and house, and fun facts about the places you pass.

  • Free: the map, miles and time left, their car on the road, music, Our Trips, the time limit and parent PIN, and Emma reading 200 built-in fun facts.
  • £2.99 a month: games and Spot-it, choosing your car and your house, and fun facts about the places you pass, written by AI and checked before your child hears them.

Who's in the car?

A little profile for each child, so the trip knows who's on board, in the £5.99 version. Their names stay on the phone.
