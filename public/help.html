Questions from the front seat

Quick answers for parents and carers. Can't find yours? Send us a message.

Getting started

What is AWTY?

A road-trip app for children. You type where you're going, lock the screen and hand it back. Children watch their car travel the map, see the miles and minutes count down, and hear Emma read fun facts along the way.

Who is it for?

Children aged about 4 to 12, in the back of the car, with a parent or carer setting it up. The driver should never use AWTY while driving.

Is it free?

Yes. The free app has the map, miles and time left, their car on the road, music, Our Trips, the time limit, the parent PIN and Emma's 200 built-in fun facts, with no sign-up and no adverts. The £2.99 a month version adds games and Spot-it, points and sound effects, choosing your car and house, and fun facts about the places you pass. See what's in each version.

How do subscriptions work?

The paid versions are monthly subscriptions through Google Play, which handles the payment. You can cancel at any time in Google Play. The free app keeps working if you do.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. AWTY is coming to Android on Google Play first.

On the road

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Choose a no-signal trip, set how long the drive is, and the car moves by the clock. Emma's fun facts are stored on the phone, so she keeps reading them.

How does the car know where we are?

Only if you let it. Location is a separate choice that starts switched off. If you switch it on, the phone's GPS moves the car along the route, on the phone, and a green "Location on" sign shows on the children's screen. With it off, the car moves by the journey time instead.

When does Emma read a fun fact?

A star appears on the map for every 15 minutes of the planned drive. When the car reaches one, a fun-fact card pops up and Emma reads it. A trip shorter than 15 minutes still gets one, halfway.

Why does Emma need Wi-Fi?

Emma's voice downloads once, by herself, the first time the phone is on Wi-Fi (about 126 MB). She never downloads during a trip. After that she works anywhere, with no signal. To use mobile data instead, tap "Download Emma now" in Settings → Sound.

Can my child get out of the trip screen?

Not without you. "Start Journey & Lock Screen" starts the trip and locks it in one go. Unlocking it needs your PIN, and while it's locked, Search, Our Trips and Settings can't be reached. If you unlock it to visit Search, Our Trips or Settings, it locks again by itself as soon as you go back to the trip.

For parents

I've forgotten my PIN

Press and hold "Forgot PIN?" for 10 seconds, then choose a new one. Holding it that long keeps little fingers out.

How does the time limit work?

In Settings → Screen → Time Limit, choose how many minutes the children can look at the trip screen on each trip (in the paid versions, how long they can play games). It's off until you choose. They get a "2 minutes left!" warning, then a friendly stop. Your PIN adds 15 more minutes.

Does AWTY show adverts or track my child?

No. There are no adverts, no tracking tools and we never sell anything about you. AWTY never asks for your children's names. Read our promise to parents.

How do I delete our data?

Almost everything stays on your phone. Settings → Parent controls → Clear everything on this phone removes it, and so does deleting the app. If you switched on "Help improve AWTY", Settings → Data & services → What we collect shows what's been sent, and "Delete my usage data" removes it from our server. Read the privacy policy.

Something isn't working

Tell us in the app, in Settings → Report a problem: it adds the app version and the kind of phone for you, so we can fix it faster. Or send us a message.

I have an idea for AWTY

We'd love to hear it. In the app, tap Settings → Suggest a feature, type your idea and tap "Send idea". Email addresses and phone numbers are taken out before it's sent. Or send us a message.

Still stuck?

Send us a message and we'll reply by email. We read every one.
